Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Filmy Camera ("we", "our", or "us") is a local-first camera app. This Privacy Policy explains what the app accesses on your iPhone or iPad, how local data is managed, and what happens when you choose to share a photo or contact support.

Photos and Camera Access

Filmy Camera requests camera access for live preview and capture. Import uses Apple's system photo picker and receives only the image you choose; importing does not require full photo-library access. Saving a finished frame requests add-only Photos access. Reading the in-app Roll is a separate, optional permission, and limited access can be managed from the Roll.

The Roll is scoped to frames created by Filmy Camera. The app does not treat every image in a similarly named album as app-owned. Denying camera access does not prevent importing and editing an existing photo.

  • Image processing happens on your device. The app does not upload photos, camera frames, or photo-library identifiers to a developer-operated server.
  • Apple Photos may download an original from iCloud or sync a saved photo according to your iCloud Photos settings. System backups may also contain app preferences or photos according to your settings.
  • When you choose Share, the photo is provided to the destination you select. That destination's handling of the photo is governed by its own policies.
  • A capped, backup-excluded local frame cache supports temporary offline Roll access. Clearing this cache does not delete originals saved in Photos.

Optional Horizon Level

When you enable Horizon level in Capture setup, Filmy Camera uses device-motion gravity values to show whether your device is level. Processing happens on your device. The app does not save or upload motion samples, and it stops motion updates when the aid is disabled or the camera leaves active use. Only your on/off preference is saved locally.

Data Retention and Deletion

Photos you save remain in Apple Photos until you delete them there or use an available app action to remove an app-created frame. Uninstalling Filmy Camera or clearing its local Roll cache does not delete those Photos originals or their iCloud copies.

The local Roll cache is temporary, storage-limited, and may be evicted by the app or operating system. Temporary share files are removed after use where possible and stale files are cleaned up on a later launch. You can clear the local Roll cache in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local container and preferences; any system backups remain subject to your Apple settings.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your photo library or an account profile for the app. Information you voluntarily send to support is separate from the app's local processing.

Third-Party Services and Diagnostics

Filmy Camera does not include an advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting, account, or subscription service. Apple provides the camera, Photos, system picker, motion, sharing, and operating-system services used by the app.

The app may record technical camera-recovery codes and retry state in local system logs. It does not send those logs to a developer-operated service or put photos, photo identifiers, filenames, or user-entered text into those camera-recovery messages. Any Apple-provided diagnostics are governed by your system settings and Apple's policies.

Support Correspondence

When you choose to email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you send through our email provider. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report. Do not include sensitive photos or other information that is not needed for support. You can ask us to delete support correspondence using the contact route below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Children's Privacy

Filmy Camera is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Controls

  • Revoke camera and Photos permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings, or manage limited Photos access from the Roll.
  • Disable Horizon level in Capture setup.
  • Clear the local Roll cache in the app's Settings without deleting Photos originals.
  • Manage saved photos, iCloud Photos, system backups, and sharing destinations through the relevant Apple or destination-app controls.
  • Uninstall Filmy Camera to remove its local app container.
  • Contact support about information you voluntarily sent us.
