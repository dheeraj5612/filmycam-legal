Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Filmy Camera ("we", "our", or "us") is a local-first camera app. This Privacy Policy explains what the app accesses on your iPhone or iPad and what it does not collect.

Information We Collect

Data Type Purpose Stored Where
Camera Live preview and capture On your device only
Photos Saving finished frames and showing frames created by Filmy Camera Apple Photos on your device; subject to your Photos/iCloud settings
Temporary local frame cache Keeping the in-app roll available when Photos access is limited On your device only; removable in Settings
Device motion and gravity Optional horizon level Processed temporarily in memory on your device; motion samples are not saved or uploaded
Recipe settings Remembering your selected look and adjustments On your device only

Photos and Camera Access

Filmy Camera requests camera access for the live preview and capture flow. It requests Photos access when you save a finished frame or choose to browse the in-app gallery. The gallery enumerates frames created by Filmy Camera; it does not treat every image in a similarly named album as app-owned.

  • Filmy Camera does not send your photos to its own servers. Photos saved to Apple Photos remain subject to your Photos/iCloud settings, backups, and any sharing or export action you choose.
  • We do not upload, analyze, or store your photos on our servers
  • All image processing happens locally on your device
  • A capped, backup-excluded local cache may be used for temporary offline roll access; you can clear it in Settings

Optional Horizon Level

When you enable Horizon level in Capture setup, Filmy Camera uses device-motion gravity values to show whether your device is level. Processing happens on your device. The app does not save or upload motion samples, and it stops motion updates when the aid is disabled or the camera leaves active use. Only your on/off preference is saved locally.

Data Retention

Photos saved through Apple Photos are governed by your Photos/iCloud settings. Filmy Camera does not retain server-side copies. The temporary frame cache and recipe settings are stored locally; system backups follow your device settings.

Third-Party Services

Filmy Camera does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, account, or subscription services. Apple provides the operating-system camera, Photos, and motion frameworks used by the app.

Children's Privacy

Filmy Camera is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete app data by uninstalling Filmy Camera
  • Revoke camera/photo permissions in iOS Settings
  • Request information about data we hold (contact us below)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
