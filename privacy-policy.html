Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 19, 2024

FilmyCam ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Data Type Purpose Stored Where
Photos & Camera Core app functionality - capturing and processing photos On your device only
Purchase History Managing premium subscriptions via Apple StoreKit Apple's servers
Crash Reports Improving app stability (via Sentry) Sentry.io servers
Device Information Debugging and analytics Anonymized, no personal data

Photos and Camera Access

FilmyCam requires camera access to capture photos and apply film effects. We also request photo library access to save your processed images.

  • Your photos never leave your device unless you explicitly share them
  • We do not upload, analyze, or store your photos on any servers
  • All image processing happens locally on your device

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not collect or store any payment information. All transactions are handled securely by Apple.

Crash Reporting (Sentry)

We use Sentry for crash reporting to improve app stability. Crash reports may include:

  • Device type and iOS version
  • App version and build number
  • Technical information about the crash
  • General usage patterns (anonymized)

Crash reports do not include any personal information, photos, or identifiable data.

Data Retention

Your photos and rolls are stored locally on your device. We do not retain any copies. Crash reports are retained by Sentry for up to 90 days for debugging purposes.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: For processing in-app purchases
  • Sentry: For crash reporting and stability monitoring

Children's Privacy

FilmyCam is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all app data by uninstalling FilmyCam
  • Revoke camera/photo permissions in iOS Settings
  • Request information about data we hold (contact us below)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
