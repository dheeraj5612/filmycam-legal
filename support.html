Filmy Camera Support

Help for the local-first camera app.

Need help? Email dheerajnamburu+support@gmail.com.

You can also open a support request on the Filmy Camera support tracker.

For either contact method, include your iOS version, device model, and a short description. Never include private photos, Apple credentials, or other sensitive information.

Common fixes

  • Camera access: open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and enable Filmy Camera.
  • Photos access: open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and choose the access level you want.
  • Roll visibility: frames saved by Filmy Camera remain in Photos; the app’s temporary local cache can be cleared from Settings inside Filmy Camera.
  • Capture review: choose Save to Photos or Retake. The camera pauses while a frame is being reviewed.
